Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026 · Applies to the iOS app "Tıp Dili" (bundle id com.tipdili.app) and its home-screen widget.

Effective dates — which part applies to you. This policy covers two versions of the app, and they handle data differently. You can see your version at the bottom of the app's Profile screen.

  • Version 1.0.1 — the version currently on the App Store (effective 2 July 2026). No accounts, no server operated by us; everything stays on your device. Part A applies to you.
  • Version 2 and later. Version 2 introduces an account (Sign in with Apple) and a backend that we operate. Part B applies, and it takes effect for you on the day you install version 2 — not before. As of the last-updated date above, version 2 has not been released.

Part C applies to both versions.

Part A — Version 1.0.1 (currently on the App Store)

A1. Data we collect

None. In version 1.0.1 the app has:

  • no user accounts, registration, or login;
  • no backend server operated by us — nothing is uploaded anywhere;
  • no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking technologies;
  • no third-party SDKs of any kind;
  • no cookies or similar identifiers (the app contains no web content);
  • no access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or health data.

A2. Data stored only on your device

To function, the app keeps a small set of preferences in Apple's standard on-device storage (the app sandbox). This data is created by you, never leaves your device, and is not accessible to us:

  • the optional display name you may enter during onboarding (used only to greet you inside the app; you may leave it blank);
  • your chosen level (beginner / intermediate / advanced), content language, learning goal, and medical-specialty interests;
  • terms you mark as favorites and your saved collections;
  • your daily progress (which terms you have seen) and streak counter;
  • reminder settings and onboarding state.

The app and its widget share these preferences through an Apple "App Group" container that also lives entirely on your device. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of this data. iOS device backups you configure (iCloud/computer) are governed by Apple, not by us.

A3. In-app purchases (Apple)

Version 1.0.1 offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, "Tıp Dili Pro". All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to display prices and to check, on your device, whether your subscription is active. In this version we operate no purchase server and hold no purchase database, and we never receive your name, address, e-mail, or payment details. Billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's Media Services terms and Apple's Privacy Policy.

A4. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. There is no push server; no message travels over the internet.

Part B — From version 2 onward

B1. What changes

Version 2 adds an account so that a Tıp Dili Pro subscription can be recognised reliably. This means that, unlike version 1.0.1, we do operate a backend and we do hold a small amount of personal data about signed-in users. Everything we hold is listed in B3 — there is nothing else. We still run no advertising, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

B2. Your account and how you sign in

  • Sign in with Apple is the only way to sign in. There is no password, no e-mail-and-password registration, and no sign-in with any other provider.
  • When you sign in, Apple sends us your e-mail address and a stable Apple identifier. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email" option, what we receive is Apple's private relay address, not your real one — that works fine with the app, and we do not try to resolve it.
  • We request only your e-mail address from Apple. We do not request or store your name from Sign in with Apple.
  • Signing in is not required to use the app. The free daily term, the widget, and reminders work without an account. An account is required for a Tıp Dili Pro subscription to be recognised, because the subscription check is made on our server.

B3. Data we collect and hold

Exactly three kinds of data, all linked to your account, none used for tracking:

  • Email — the address Apple provides at sign-in (which may be an Apple private relay address). Used to identify your account and, if necessary, to contact you about it.
  • UserID — the account identifier we generate for you, plus the identifiers needed to match your subscription. Used to link your device session to your account.
  • PurchaseHistory — subscription status and the purchase events that produce it (start, renewal, expiry, cancellation), as reported to us by the payment infrastructure. Used solely to decide whether Pro is active for you.

This is the same disclosure we make on the App Store product page: these three data types are linked to you and not used to track you. We collect no other category — no location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, or advertising data.

B4. What still stays on your device

The learning side of the app remains local. Terms, definitions, and examples are bundled inside the app; your favorites, collections, daily progress, streak, level, language, interests, and reminder settings continue to live only on your device and are not uploaded to us. Studying, quizzes, practice, and the widget keep working offline. What needs a connection is signing in and checking your subscription.

B5. Infrastructure we use

  • Supabase (managed PostgreSQL and authentication) runs the account database and the sign-in session.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription events so that entitlement status stays correct across renewals and restores.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and, as in version 1.0.1, all payment. We never receive your payment details.
Where this data is stored geographically, and the data-processing agreements covering these providers, are being finalised. We will publish those details on this page before version 2 is released. Until they appear here, treat this section as naming the technology we use, not as a statement about data location or international transfers.

B6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: open Profile → Account & Data, open your account, then choose Request account deletion and confirm. There is no need to e-mail us and no waiting on a support queue. What happens:

  • Your account is disabled immediately and your sessions are ended. From that moment the server answers "no access" for your account, so Pro features stop being unlocked through it.
  • Your record is then removed from the subscription infrastructure, and once that is confirmed, your account and all data linked to it — e-mail, account identifiers, subscription records — are permanently deleted from our database in a single step. This is not reversible.
  • What remains afterwards is only an identity-free record that a deletion happened (timestamps and counts). It contains no e-mail address, no identifier, and no hash of either, so it cannot be linked back to you.
  • Deletion is not reversible. Signing in with Apple again later creates a new, empty account.
  • Data stored on your device (favorites, progress, preferences) is not affected by account deletion — remove it by deleting the app.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription. Your subscription is a contract between you and Apple, and only you can cancel it, in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tıp Dili Pro → Cancel. If you delete your account without cancelling, Apple will keep charging you. Cancel first, then delete.

B7. How long we keep data

Account data (B3) is kept for as long as your account exists, and is deleted when you delete your account, as described in B6. Records of subscription events that carry no identifier are kept for at most 90 days for reconciliation and then deleted. E-mail correspondence follows C2.

B8. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) / KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): creating and maintaining your account and determining your subscription entitlement.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) / KVKK Art. 5(2)(a)): keeping records we are required to keep, and handling your data-subject requests.
  • Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) / KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)): keeping the service secure and preventing abuse of subscriptions.

We do not process your data for advertising, and we do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal effects for you.

Part C — Applies to both versions

C1. Who is responsible ("data controller")

The app is published by Mert Kaya Özkan (the "developer", "we"). For anything in this policy you can reach us at iletisim@alandili.com.

C2. E-mail correspondence

If you contact us at the address above, we will process the personal data you include (your e-mail address and message) solely to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) / communication initiated by you (KVKK Art. 5). Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and is then deleted upon request or when no longer necessary. It is never used for marketing and never shared.

C3. Children

The app is an educational dictionary suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process children's personal data; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it. Purchases are controlled by Apple's parental controls (Screen Time / Ask to Buy).

C4. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK Art. 11), you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, and port your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK; in the EU/EEA: your local DPA).

  • Version 1.0.1: we hold no data about app users, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase; on-device data is erased by deleting the app. Rights over e-mail correspondence (C2) still apply.
  • Version 2 and later: the data in B3 is subject to all of these rights. The fastest route to erasure is the in-app deletion in B6, which is immediate and needs no request. For access, rectification, or a copy of your data, write to us at the address in C1; we respond without undue delay and in any case within one month (GDPR) / thirty days (KVKK).

C5. Security

On-device preferences live inside the iOS app sandbox and are protected by your device's security (passcode/Face ID, encryption at rest as provided by iOS). From version 2, account data is held in a managed database with access restricted at the row level, so a signed-in session can reach only its own account's records; connections are encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but the amount of personal data we hold is deliberately kept to the minimum listed in B3.

C6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date before the change takes effect in the app. Material changes affecting a version you already use will be pointed out in the app.

C7. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: iletisim@alandili.com
